## Main menu and settings

- Added a main menu with **START GAME** and **SETTINGS**.
- Added a persistent master-volume slider stored in `user://settings.cfg`.
- Added an in-game **SETTINGS** button at the bottom-right of the side panel.
- Added **END GAME** to in-game settings only. It is hidden when settings are opened from the main menu.
- **END GAME** opens the regular game-over score panel.
- Added a **MAIN MENU** button to end-of-round and game-over panels.

## Team controls and UI

- Removed the per-team dropdown action rows.
- Enlarged the **YOUR TEAMS** section and made team rows visibly clickable.
- Added shared centered **STOP**, **DISPATCH**, and **CLEAR** buttons.
- Preserved click-and-drag route planning on the map.
- Team selection remains highlighted when a team is moving or has already been dispatched.
- Moving teams now show their team name and STOP instruction instead of "No team selected".
- Added five distinct team colors for Teams A-E.
- Limited generated gameplay to a maximum of five teams.
- Removed the separate bottom-right **People Lost** module.

## Round endings and scoring

- Automatic game over from casualties occurs only when deaths exceed all survivors still alive in the round.
- If no safe route remains, the next round is available only when saved people strictly outnumber lost people; equal saved/lost numbers are game over.
- When no safe route remains, survivors in waiting or blocked groups are counted as trapped/lost before the ending decision.
- No-safe-route endings use the full score breakdown panel.
- Added **VIEW MAP**, **NEXT ROUND**, and **MAIN MENU** choices to the no-route ending.
- Viewing the map now exposes a **ROUND OPTIONS** button that reopens the ending panel.
- Updated the score breakdown text to match the new round-completion rule.
- Added regression coverage for trapped groups when no safe route remains, including the equal saved/lost game-over case.
